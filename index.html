    KEEP PROJECTS ORGANISED

    Start personal work in your home directory.

    Your personal $HOME is shared across all three nodes. Use it for project files, logs, and results; leave Enroot's working caches at their system defaults. Ask Josh about managed storage under /home/data and which nodes can access a project directory.

    What is on the system?¶

    Component What it is for
    DGX Spark NVIDIA GPU compute for research workloads
    Slurm Requests resources and runs interactive or batch jobs
    Enroot + Pyxis Runs containers directly through Slurm without sudo

    Need help?¶

    If a job fails or you need an account, contact Josh Bamforth at josh.bamforth@shu.ac.uk. The FAQ & Troubleshooting page covers common job and container issues.
